Drag! The Musical

Off-Broadway campaign copywriting

I developed creative concepts and copy for the marketing campaign for off-Broadway show Drag! The Musical, including commercial scripts, digital banners and signage, email marketing, website copy, organic and paid social copy, flyers, and street team talking points.

I also developed cross-promotional concepts with other off-Broadway shows, and ghostwrote a letter from Liza Minnelli (producer of the show)!
