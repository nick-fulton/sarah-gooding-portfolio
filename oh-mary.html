Oh, Mary!

Broadway copywriting

Oh, Mary! is a critically acclaimed Broadway show created by and starring Cole Escola.

As a Copywriter with the agency RPM, I worked on marketing and advertising for the show, which is currently in performances at the Lyceum Theatre.

I wrote banner ads, email marketing copy, OOH, promotional print material, and more.

The show and its marketing have been wildly successful and the production has already been extended numerous times.
