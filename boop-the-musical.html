BOOP! The Musical

Broadway copywriting

BOOP! The Musical is a new Broadway show that brings the famous cartoon character Betty Boop to the live stage for the first time ever.

As a Copywriter with the agency RPM, I created marketing and advertising assets in the lead-up to the show's debut at the Broadhurst Theatre.

I created promotional video scripts, banner ads, paid social videos, web copy, a children's entertainment guide, email marketing, and more.
